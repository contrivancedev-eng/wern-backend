Step-based Rewards: The foundational reward mechanism where users earn "Litties" (the basic unit currency) for walking. Conversion rate: 1,000 steps = 1 Littie. Users earn through real-time step tracking using device motion sensors (accelerometer) and GPS integration. The system includes fraud prevention mechanisms with device attestation and improbable speed/acceleration detection. Litties can be converted to impact coins or redeemed for goods, services, or social impact.
